## Design specificity verdict

**Review:** half authored, half template. The chrome has real FFXIV character: Cinzel, gold on
near-black, corner-bracketed cards, the diamond glyph, Grand Company palettes. Under it, every
page is the same stack of framed cards with a title, two pills and a gold button. Because every
card gets the same brackets, ornament stops marking what matters. The biggest miss is
Exchanges: 33 text rows for mounts, minions and barding, with no item art. The thing players
are farming toward is invisible.

**Detector:** the CLI found 1 issue: `bounce-easing` at `app/src/index.css:132` (`--ease-pop`).
It is deliberate overshoot from the motion layer, so it's a judgement call, not an accident. The
browser overlay found more on overview, exchanges and settings (objectives and planner were
clean):

- `undersized-ui-text` (10.5px) and `tiny-text` (11px) on the HUD tomestone label and the
  "No goal yet" line. Same issue the review flagged, counted twice. Real.
- `nested-cards`: the HUD framed card inside its own strip. Agrees with the review. Real.
- `all-caps-body` on 34 and 35 characters: the uppercase tomestone name in the HUD. Short
  label, so a soft hit, but it also truncates on phone.
- `layout-transition` (`transition: width`) twice: the progress bar fill. Real perf hit, low
  severity; animate `transform: scaleX` instead.

The detector did not catch the truncation, missing item art or redundant wallet. The review
did.

## Overall impression

The core works. Run Next answers "what should I run next?" in one card, and that is the
product. Around it, the app repeats the same framed row on every page, hides the rewards and
spends too much phone height on chrome. The biggest opportunity is to make the rewards visible
and let Run Next lead.

## What's working

1. **Run Next** (`app/src/components/RunNext.tsx`). Ranked rows with tome count, effort and a
   single action. It is the product principle made literal.
2. **Token system.** Per-palette dark and light tokens (`app/src/index.css:3-89`) with a contrast
   test behind them. The gold Cinzel wallet number is the right hero.
3. **Mobile shell.** Labelled bottom tab bar in thumb reach, and a sticky HUD so the wallet is
   always visible.

## Priority issues

**[P1] Exchanges hides the reward and offers no way to find an item**
- Why: "never miss an item you wanted" is half the purpose. 33 art-less rows, an unlabelled
  diamond toggle, and chosen items that look disabled.
- Fix: item thumbnails or type glyphs, a labelled Want control, search or a type filter,
  wishlisted names at full contrast with a gold edge. Put Want and Mark exchanged on a stable
  second row on phone.
- Command: `/impeccable layout`, then `/impeccable bolder`.

**[P1] Truncated titles hide the choice itself**
- Why: Run Next and Planner cut titles to "Wk 1: 5 Bozja skirmishes or ca…" and "randomly
  assig…". The title is the answer the app exists to give.
- Fix: `line-clamp-2` instead of `truncate` at `RunNext.tsx:42`, `PlannerPage.tsx:55,84` and
  the Exchanges title rows. Move the action below the title at 390px if needed.
- Command: `/impeccable adapt`.

**[P2] Overview repeats the wallet and the HUD eats the phone**
- Why: the first glance on a phone is HUD plus a duplicated number. The tomestone label is 10.5px
  caps and truncates; the "No goal yet" hint is 11px.
- Fix: one-line HUD on phone (event, bar, count). One large wallet on Overview. Drop the nested
  frame. Hints at 13px or more, linked to Exchanges.
- Command: `/impeccable distill`.

**[P2] No first run**
- Why: a new player sees a zero wallet, an empty wishlist and a Run Next ranked against no
  goal, with no path from there.
- Fix: a 3-step first-run card (set wallet, wishlist an item, run next) that goes away once
  done. Say "saved on this device" in Settings.
- Command: `/impeccable onboard`.

**[P3] Objectives filter overload and silent logging**
- Why: 11 filter chips wrap to 3 lines on phone. "Did it" gives no sense of what was earned.
- Fix: a horizontally scrolling chip row or a type select. A brief "+4" cue with undo.
- Command: `/impeccable delight`.

## Persona red flags

**Daily farmer (power user):** one tap per objective with no quick-log. No search over 33
exchanges. Planner is a separate route from Run Next. No progress export, so no backup. The
11-chip filter takes 3 rows.

**First-time event player:** the only guidance is 11px "No goal yet". The Want diamond is
unlabelled and the dimmed name after wishlisting looks like a bug. Run Next does not say what
it ranks against when the wishlist is empty.

**Phone user mid-queue:** HUD takes about 110 of 844px. Truncated Run Next and Planner titles.
Wallet stepper buttons (-10, -1, +1, +10) are tight. The Want tooltip covers the item name on
tap. Objectives is about 2400px of scroll. "Did it" and reset sit close together.

## Minor observations

- Desktop is a 960px column in a lot of empty dark space. Exchanges could use two columns.
- "1 TOMES" on the Magicked Prism row should be "1 TOME".
- Settings has no progress management.
- Light, Adder and Flames palettes still need a visual check.
- The tome art is a placeholder. A bigger hero image would expose that, so replacing it is a
  prerequisite for any tome-forward redesign.

## Questions to consider

1. If Run Next is the best screen, why is it the third card on Overview instead of the screen?
2. What if Exchanges were a shelf of item art with "x tomes short" under each item?
3. Do three Grand Company palettes earn a settings group in a glance tool, or would one strong
   identity serve better?
